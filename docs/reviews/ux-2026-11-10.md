# UX review, 2026-11-10

Reviewed against commit `19b8dae` (Phases 0 to 8 and 7b) and all 38 screenshots in `shots-drive`, `shots-board`, and `shots-notes`. The lens is user experience (UX): flows, structure, discoverability, friction, recovery, and keyboard paths. Visual design is out of scope except where it breaks a flow.

Terms: **Mod** is Ctrl on Windows and Linux, Cmd on macOS. Effort: **S** is under half a day, **M** is one to three days, **L** is a week or more. File paths are under `ui/src/` unless they start with `core/`, `app/`, or `docs/`. `b6-nudged.png` shows focus and selection on different cards; commit `ec1b681` fixed that, so it is not counted here.

## Verdict

1. The foundation is strong: the shell, the palette, the relation line, Undo notices, and the board's keyboard model are better than most comparable tools.
2. The main flow has three gaps: no `[[` suggestions while writing, no way to record a relation on a board, and a board that loses its pan and zoom when you open an entry.
3. Verbs and qualifier labels cannot be fixed at all, and a few single-key paths lose data with no Undo (tag merge, Escape in a note, board delete).
4. Most fixes are small (S) and follow the design's own rules; 14 failing items need an owner decision, each with a recommended option.
5. Do the top ten before Phase 9, because import will add tags and verbs that the app cannot yet tidy.

## Succeeding

- **The three-column shell keeps its shape.** Column widths do not change between Read, Edit, and other views. The sidebar and the panel fold to remembered rails. Evidence: `App.svelte` lines 334-361, `components/EntryPanel.svelte` lines 53-57, `3-edit.png` against `4-read.png`.
- **The relation line shows its own steps.** It has a visible label, a stage hint (verb, target, qualifiers), suggestions in the page flow, verb suggestions on focus with an empty field, "(new)" on words and entries that will be created, Backspace to unstage, and one transaction on save. Evidence: `components/RelationBuilder.svelte` lines 110-218 and 372-419, `2-oak-new.png`, `b0-entry.png`.
- **Reversible by default.** Move to Trash has no confirm, offers Undo, and replaces the history entry so Back does not land on a gone entry. Board removals offer Undo and tie lost strings again. Evidence: `views/EntryView.svelte` lines 299-321, `views/BoardView.svelte` lines 334-365 and 492-523, `n9-deleted.png`, `n10-undone.png`.
- **Rename keeps links whole.** Links are rewritten, the old title becomes an alias, and Undo works from the notice and from More for the session. Evidence: `views/EntryView.svelte` lines 224-264.
- **One keyboard map, shown where it is used.** `lib/keys.ts` drives the key handler, the palette rows, and the tooltips. The webview's own F5, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+P, and Ctrl+F are off. Evidence: `lib/keys.ts` lines 31-43, `App.svelte` lines 294-314, `app/src/main.rs` lines 211-235.
- **The palette reaches entries, commands, and guides from one field.** Entry rows show tag paths, command rows show shortcuts, a last row creates the typed entry, and `>` limits the list to commands. Evidence: `lib/palette.ts` lines 44-78, `10-palette.png`.
- **The board works from the keyboard for single items.** Focus selects; arrows nudge; Shift moves farther; Alt with arrows resizes; Enter opens or edits; Delete removes with Undo; the pin ties a string from the keyboard with a status line; with the canvas focused, arrows pan and plus and minus zoom. Most whiteboard tools need a pointer for this. Evidence: `components/BoardCanvas.svelte` lines 280-305 and 537-657, `n4-tying.png`.
- **Fixed action rows instead of hover controls.** The relation list, Trash, and the Tag editor act on a selected row from one fixed place. Evidence: `components/RelationList.svelte` lines 226-236, `8-trash.png`, `6-tags.png`.
- **Autosave states are exact.** "Unsaved", "Saved", or "Save failed" with Retry. Leaving the page writes the draft; a failure after leaving becomes a notice that names the entry and offers "Open entry". Evidence: `views/EntryView.svelte` lines 136-208 and 456-467.
- **Empty states name the next step and its key.** Evidence: `views/EntryView.svelte` line 551, `views/GuideView.svelte` line 300, `views/BoardView.svelte` lines 812-816, `2-oak-new.png`, `b2-empty-board.png`.
- **Screenshot capture works on entry pages.** Paste lands at the caret in Edit and at the end of the body in Read. The first image becomes the card picture. The viewer and the album work from the keyboard. Evidence: `views/EntryView.svelte` lines 372-424, `3b-paste.png`.
- **Board notes work as task lists.** Check boxes flip the markdown, notes resize like Lucidchart's, and colors, labeled strings, and backgrounds work. Evidence: `n1-note.png` to `n8-grid.png`.
- **Confirm dialogs name the thing and the result.** The confirming button carries the verb, never "OK". Evidence: `views/TagsView.svelte` lines 457-464, `components/ConfirmDialog.svelte`.
- **The previous review mostly landed.** Of its 29 ranked changes, 21 landed in full, 3 in part, and 3 not at all; 1 is scheduled later and 1 was replaced by manual boards. The gaps are listed in Failing 22. Evidence: `docs/ux-review.md` section 6 against the source.

## Failing

Ranked by impact on building a guide while playing.

1. **No link suggestions while writing.**
   - Problem: Typing `[[` in the editor or in a board note offers no titles, so every link depends on memory and exact spelling.
   - Where: `components/Editor.svelte` lines 30-58 (no autocomplete extension); `components/BoardCanvas.svelte` lines 859-866 (notes are a plain text area); `3-edit.png` (an unresolved `[[Cobblestone]]` that only the preview reveals).
   - Who and when: The owner writes "Zombie drops [[Roten Flesh]]" during play. The only signal is a dashed underline in the preview pane. Later a click on that link creates a misspelled second entry for one thing.
   - Fix: Add `@codemirror/autocomplete` with a source that opens on `[[`, queries `api.suggestNames` (titles and aliases, stubs marked), inserts `Title]]` on Enter, and ends with a "Create entry" row. Use the same list in board notes.
   - Effort: M.
   - Decision: None. The package is bundled and makes no network request.

2. **The board cannot record a relation, and its panel says it can.**
   - Problem: The selected-card panel lists relations but has no relation line, and its empty text tells the user to "Type a verb above".
   - Where: `components/BoardPanel.svelte` lines 213-231; `components/RelationList.svelte` line 199; `n4-tying.png`.
   - Who and when: On a "Crypt" board the owner sees that the Silver Key opens the Crypt Door. To record it they must choose Open entry, which leaves the board, add the relation, press Back, and find their place again (item 3).
   - Fix: Render `RelationBuilder` at the top of the selected-card section, and let Mod+R focus it on a board. Give `RelationList` an empty-text property so the board says "No relations yet. Add one above."
   - Effort: M. The copy alone is S.
   - Decision: Options: (a) relation line in the board panel; (b) copy fix only. Recommended: (a). Design section 6 says boards are where puzzles unfold with the entries' help, and the new edge is drawn at once.

3. **Leaving a board loses the view, and reading an entry requires leaving.**

   - Effort: S for the view. M for the body.
   - Decision: For the body: (a) two tabs in the selected-card section, "Entry" first and "Relations" second; (b) the body above the relations in one scroll. Recommended: (a), because relations between members are already drawn on the canvas.

## Missing

Ranked by value for building a guide while playing.

1. **Board and entry in both directions.**
   - Reference: Obsidian Canvas ("Convert to file", adding linked files); Heptabase (a card lists the whiteboards it is on).
   - Fit: In the selected-card panel, each relation whose other end is not on the board gets "Add", plus "Add related (n)". These cards land around the selected card; the middle-of-window rule in design section 6 was written for the picker, which has no anchor. A note gets "Make this note an entry": the note becomes a card at the same place, its text becomes the body, and its strings stay. A string between two cards gets "Make this a relation": the relation line opens with both ends and the label as the verb, and the string is removed with Undo once the edge exists. The entry page lists "On boards" with links and offers "Add to board...".
   - Effort: M. Needs a decision on placement around the card and on string promotion.

2. **Backlinks with context, and unlinked mentions.**
   - Reference: Obsidian's backlinks pane; Roam's linked and unlinked references.
   - Fit: "Mentioned by" rows show the sentence around each link. A second fold, "Unlinked mentions (n)", lists bodies that contain the title or an alias as plain text. "Link" wraps that occurrence in `[[ ]]`, which is an ordinary body edit. Core's full-text search (FTS) index already holds titles, aliases, and bodies.
   - Effort: M.

3. **A facts box in Read mode.**
   - Reference: Fandom and MediaWiki infoboxes; Capacities and Anytype show an object's properties at its top.
   - Fit: A read-only box at the top right of the body with the primary image and the relation groups (verb heading, linked targets). The panel stays the place to edit. It answers "what is this thing" without a click, which matters while Failing 10 stands.
   - Effort: M. Needs a decision: a second, read-only display of relations beside the panel.

4. **Keep the verb after saving a relation.**
   - Reference: Tana and Capacities fields that take several values.
   - Fit: After Save, the line keeps the verb chip and waits for the next target; Backspace or Escape clears it. "Zombie DROPS Rotten Flesh, Iron Ingot, Carrot" then needs the verb once. Today `staged = EMPTY` resets it (`components/RelationBuilder.svelte` line 359).
   - Effort: S.

   - Fit: With an empty field, the palette lists the last eight opened entries instead of the first eight by title (`components/Palette.svelte` lines 134-147; `core/src/entry.rs` line 316). Launch reopens the last route, and each guide remembers its last entry. This is stored in `config.toml`, inside the data directory.
   - Effort: S.

   - Fit: Today the guide page repeats the sidebar's list (`1-guide.png`). Sections instead: Recently edited; To describe (stubs); Wanted (unresolved link targets with counts and Create); Boards (with open tasks); then the full table.

   - Effort: M to L. Needs a decision, because the data model and the export format change.

    - Effort: S to M.

## Do not do

- **A global graph view** (Obsidian, Logseq). Force layouts move by themselves and turn hundreds of nodes into noise. The hand-filled board is the decided alternative (design section 6).
- **Hover previews of links** (Obsidian's page preview, Wikipedia's page previews). Content that appears on hover breaks "no control appears or disappears on hover" (design section 5) and pulls attention. Use a fixed place: the board panel or the entry tabs.
- **Relations typed inline in the body** (Dataview's `key:: value`, Logseq properties). One edge would have two sources, and design section 4 keeps mentions and relations apart on purpose.
- **Block references and transclusion** (Roam, Logseq). Block ids would enter the markdown and spoil the export that people read without the app.
- **Database grids of properties** (Notion). They repeat what the relation line and tags already store.
- **Automatic layout of a whole board.** It moves hand-placed work and is motion. Wider spacing for new cards (Failing 13) is enough.
- **Silent conversion between strings and relations.** A string is a guess; a relation is a fact. Promotion must be an explicit action (Missing 1).
- **A system-wide capture hotkey.** It hooks the keyboard while the window is in the background, collides with game keys, and conflicts with "nothing runs in the background".
- **Split panes or tabs for several entries.** They change column widths and placement, which design section 5 fixes. The board and the board panel cover side-by-side reading.
- **Journal pages as the home** (Logseq, Roam). They turn a reference wiki into a diary. Session notes belong to the planned scratchpad under its own name.
- **Animated feedback** (sliding notices, smooth zoom). The design forbids animation.
